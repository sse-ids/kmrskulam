About Our School

KMRS Kulam is a CBSE Affiliated English Medium School running under National Education Society for Tribal Students, ST & SC Development, Minorities & Backward Classes Welfare Department, Govt of Odisha. Its vision is catalyzing socio-economic development of the most underprivileged groups in Odisha i.e. Scheduled Tribes (STs) in a coordinated and planned manner by imparting Quality Education & using it as an effective instrument for their holistic empowerment.
We, all stakeholders of KMRS Kulam, are trying our best to realize this vision. As a School Leader my role is to work collaboratively with all stakeholders to consciously shift towards making learning a part of the school's culture and ethos. The effort for shifting towards learning by everyone in the system are in terms of:

1. learning to know - Principal & Teachers need to continuously upgrade their knowledge of pedagogical practice, content, and school leadership.
2. learning to do - Principal & Teachers need to continuously upgrade their skills.
3. learning to live together - Principal & Teachers need to build a culture of collaboration in school for realizing its vision.
4. learning to be - Principal & Teachers need to work towards enhancing their wellbeing as community of learners.
It's a collective mission on the part of all stake holders of KMRS Kulam to provide quality education to the students in the process of nation building.
