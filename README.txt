DEMO GOVERNMENT SCHOOL WEBSITE
================================

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. For best results, upload the complete folder to your web hosting.

PAGES
- index.html (Home)
- about.html
- gallery.html
- academics.html
- events.html
- achievements.html
- notices.html
- tenders.html
- contact.html

CUSTOMISE BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace the demo logo (the circular D in the header) with your school logo.
2. Replace the demo school name, address, phone number and email in every HTML page.
3. Replace demo photos / image URLs with your own school photos.
4. Update teacher names, designations and portrait photos in index.html.
5. Update notice/tender dates and titles, then replace the PDFs in assets/pdf/.
6. In contact.html, replace the Google Maps iframe query and directions link with your exact school location.
7. Update the developer credit in the footer.
8. Update school timings, curriculum and page content with verified official information.

TECHNOLOGY
Pure HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework or backend is required.
Google Fonts, Google Maps and demo photos need an internet connection.
